# IEEE 802.11p DSRC Wireless Model

The **IEEE 802.11p DSRC** module implements the physical layer and Medium Access Control (MAC) layer of Dedicated Short-Range Communications (DSRC) / ITS-G5 over 10 MHz channels in the 5.9 GHz ITS band.

It provides high-fidelity parity with **ns-3 v3.42** and **OMNeT++ Veins 5.2**.

---

## 1. Physical Layer (PHY) Models

The PHY model simulates radio wave propagation, obstacle attenuation, diffraction around occluding corners, cumulative interference, and frame error decoding.

### 1.1. Dual-Slope Log-Distance Path Loss
In vehicular environments, radio waves reflect off ground asphalt and roadside barriers, producing a distinct two-slope path loss curve with a characteristic breakpoint distance.

Implemented in [`DualSlopePathLoss`](../../src/phy/path_loss.rs):

- **Carrier Frequency**: $f_c = 5.9\,\text{GHz}$.
- **Reference Loss at $d_0 = 1\,\text{m}$**:

  $$PL_0 = 20 \log_{10}\left(\frac{4 \pi \cdot 1.0 \cdot f_c}{c}\right) \approx 47.85\,\text{dB}$$

- **Breakpoint Distance ($d_{break}$)**: $120.0\,\text{m}$.
- **Near-field Path Loss Exponent ($\gamma_1$)**: $2.05$.
- **Far-field Path Loss Exponent ($\gamma_2$)**: $3.90$.

$$\text{PL}_{det}(d) = \begin{cases} 
PL_0 + 10 \gamma_1 \log_{10}(d), & d \le d_{break} \\ 
PL_0 + 10 \gamma_1 \log_{10}(d_{break}) + 10 \gamma_2 \log_{10}\left(\frac{d}{d_{break}}\right), & d > d_{break} 
\end{cases}$$

### 1.2. Log-Normal Shadow Fading
Log-normal shadow fading models slow signal variations caused by foliage and topography:

$$X_\sigma \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$$

- Line-of-Sight ($\text{LoS}$): $\sigma_{LoS} = 3.1\,\text{dB}$.
- Non-Line-of-Sight ($\text{NLoS}$): $\sigma_{NLoS} = 8.3\,\text{dB}$.

### 1.3. Building Obstacle Penetration Attenuation
Using the building polygons parsed from SUMO or OpenStreetMap (`osm.poly.xml`), the engine performs 2D ray-casting:

- Calculates the total internal distance $d_{pen}$ traversed through concrete walls.
- Attenuation: $0.85\,\text{dB/m}$ up to a maximum cap of $45.0\,\text{dB}$:

  $$\text{PL}_{pen} = \min(d_{pen} \times 0.85\,\text{dB/m}, 45.0\,\text{dB})$$

### 1.4. ITU-R P.526 Knife-Edge Corner Diffraction
When buildings occlude the direct line of sight between two vehicles, radio signals diffract around the closest building corner.

Implemented in [`FresnelKnifeEdge`](../../src/phy/knife_edge.rs):

1. **Fresnel-Kirchhoff Parameter $\nu$**:

   $$\nu = h \sqrt{\frac{2(d_1 + d_2)}{\lambda d_1 d_2}}$$

   where $h$ is the clearance height, $\lambda = c / f_c \approx 0.0508\,\text{m}$, and $d_1, d_2$ are the distances from transmitter and receiver to the corner vertex.
2. **Diffraction Loss $J(\nu)$**:

   $$J(\nu) = \begin{cases}
   0\,\text{dB}, & \nu \le -0.78 \\
   \left[ 6.9 + 20 \log_{10}\left(\sqrt{(\nu - 0.1)^2 + 1} + \nu - 0.1\right) \right]_{0}^{45\,\text{dB}}, & \nu > -0.78
   \end{cases}$$

$$\text{Received Power: } P_{rx} = P_{tx} - (\text{PL}_{det}(d) + X_\sigma + \text{PL}_{pen} + J(\nu))$$

## 2. Interference Tracking & Reception Decider

Implemented in [`CumulativeInterferenceTracker`](../../src/phy/interference_helper.rs), providing strict architectural equivalence to ns-3 v3.42 `InterferenceHelper`:

### 2.1. Piecewise Constant SINR Chunking
A packet reception interval $[t_{start}, t_{end}]$ is sliced into distinct temporal chunks at every boundary where concurrent interferers start or stop transmitting:

- In each chunk $i$, total interference power $I_i$ is summed in linear Watts:

  $$I_i = \sum_{j \in \text{active interferers}} P_{rx, j}(t_{mid})$$

  $$\text{SINR}_i = \frac{P_{rx, desired}}{I_i + N_0}$$

  *(where thermal noise floor $N_0 = -99.0\,\text{dBm} \approx 1.259 \times 10^{-13}\,\text{W}$).*

### 2.2. Receiver Capture Effect ($\gamma_{cap} = 4.0\,\text{dB}$)
If interfering signals overlap with the desired packet:

- Evaluates the Signal-to-Interference Ratio: $\text{SIR}_i = \frac{P_{rx, desired}}{I_i}$.
- **Destructive collision**: If $\text{SIR}_i < 4.0\,\text{dB}$ and $\text{SINR}_i < 6.0\,\text{dB}$, capture fails and the packet is lost ($\text{PSR} = 0$).
- **Successful capture**: If $\text{SIR}_i \ge 4.0\,\text{dB}$, the receiver locks onto the desired signal despite the simultaneous interferer.

### 2.3. Analytical Packet Error Rate (PER) Curve
For each chunk, the PER for IEEE 802.11p OFDM 6 Mbps (QPSK 1/2 with convolutional coding) is computed using the ns-3 `NistErrorRateModel` logistic curve:

$$\text{PER}_i(\text{SINR}_i) = \begin{cases}
1.0, & \text{SINR}_{i,\text{dB}} < 2.0\,\text{dB} \\
0.0, & \text{SINR}_{i,\text{dB}} > 12.0\,\text{dB} \\
\min\left(1.0, \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left(1.6 (\text{SINR}_{i,\text{dB}} - 5.5)\right)} \times \text{clamp}\left(\frac{L_{bytes}}{300}, 0.5, 3.0\right)\right), & 2.0 \le \text{SINR}_{i,\text{dB}} \le 12.0
\end{cases}$$

The total Packet Success Rate is the product across all chunks:

$$\text{PSR} = \prod_i (1 - \text{PER}_i)$$

## 3. MAC Layer & EDCA Contention

Implemented in [`EdcaChannelAccess`](../../src/mac/edca.rs) according to IEEE 802.11-2016 / 802.11p:

### 3.1. 4 Access Categories (EDCA ACs)
| Access Category | Constant | Traffic Type | $AIFSN$ | $CW_{min}$ | $CW_{max}$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **`VoiceSafety`** | `AC_VO` (0) | Emergency / High-Priority Safety (DENM) | **2** | **3** | **7** |
| **`Video`** | `AC_VI` (1) | High-throughput video / sensor sharing | **3** | **7** | **15** |
| **`BestEffort`** | `AC_BE` (2) | Standard periodic Cooperative Awareness (CAM) | **6** | **15** | **1023** |
| **`Background`** | `AC_BK` (3) | Non-safety background telemetry | **9** | **15** | **1023** |

### 3.2. Timing Parameters
- **Slot Time ($\sigma$)**: $13\,\mu\text{s}$.
- **SIFS**: $32\,\mu\text{s}$.
- **AIFS**: $AIFS[AC] = SIFS + AIFSN[AC] \times \sigma$.
- **Backoff Delay**: $AIFS[AC] + \text{UniformRandom}(0, CW) \times \sigma$.
- **Air Duration**: $40\,\mu\text{s} + \lceil(16 + 8 L_{bytes} + 6)/24\rceil \times 8\,\mu\text{s}$ at 6 Mbps.

### 3.3. Internal Contention Resolution
When multiple queues within the same node have frames ready at the same time:

$$\text{VoiceSafety} > \text{Video} > \text{BestEffort} > \text{Background}$$

The winning queue transmits. Subordinate colliding queues double their contention window ($CW \leftarrow \min(2 \times CW + 1, CW_{max})$) and re-enter backoff.

### 3.4. Channel Monitor (CBR) & DCC
[`ChannelMonitor`](../../src/mac/channel_monitor.rs) calculates the true union of channel busy intervals over a sliding $100\,\text{ms}$ window:

$$\text{CBR} = \frac{T_{busy}}{100\,\text{ms}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$$

The measured CBR is fed every 100 ms into Decentralized Congestion Control (DCC Reactive FSM or LIMERIC) to regulate CAM generation intervals ($T_{off}$).
